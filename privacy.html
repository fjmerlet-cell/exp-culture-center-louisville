Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how eXp Culture Center Louisville ("we," "us") collects, uses and protects information from the real estate agents who are members of our office community, through our website, our member app and our text message program.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us: name, email address, mobile phone number and preferred language.
  • Membership information: your brokerage role, the events you RSVP to, rooms you book and messages you send inside the member app.
  • Inquiries you submit through the contact form on our website.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit, such as device type, used to keep the site working and secure.

How we use it

  • To send you reminders and updates about trainings, classes, events and office announcements, by email, app notification and text message.
  • To run the member app: your profile in the office directory, room bookings, event check-in and messaging.
  • To answer questions you send us.

Text messages

You give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages when you create your member profile in our app, or when you sign our paper member form at the office. Messages are reminders about trainings, classes, events and office announcements. Message frequency varies, usually a few messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that operate our systems on our behalf — our database and app hosting provider, our email delivery provider and our text message provider — and only so they can deliver these services to you. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your member information while you are a member of the Culture Center, and for a reasonable period afterward for our records. Ask us to delete your information at any time and we will.

Your choices

  • Turn email reminders off in the member app, under Notifications, or use the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop texts.
  • Turn app notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

Security

Member information is stored in a password-protected database with access limited to Culture Center administrators. Signing in requires a one-time code sent to your email address; we do not store passwords.

Children

Our services are for licensed real estate professionals and are not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the updated version on this page with a new date.
